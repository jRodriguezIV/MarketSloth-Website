What MarketSloth does · How it works

Less FOMO
More clarity when it matters.

MarketSloth is a market-monitoring and alert platform that turns market data into clear trade setups, helping you stay informed without spending your day watching charts.

01 / We watch

Market monitoring, handled.

Our strategies analyze price movement and technical indicators across supported stocks and ETFs, looking for conditions that meet a defined setup.

02 / You get the context

A setup you can evaluate.

When a setup qualifies, MarketSloth sends an alert with its direction, strategy, price targets, and stop loss. You can review the opportunity and its boundaries together.

03 / You stay in control

Your attention. Your decision.

Decide whether the setup fits your own plan and risk limits. Take action through your broker or let it pass, with the final decision always yours.

We do the watching. You make the call. Less screen time, clearer information, and more room for the rest of your day.

Beginner-friendly by design

Built to be clear at a glance.

Each MarketSloth signal brings the setup, price levels, strategy, and supporting market context together in one view — enough information to understand what was detected without spending your day studying charts.

  • 1

    Know exactly what was flagged

    The signal direction and ticker immediately show you which market opportunity MarketSloth detected.

  • 2

    See the opportunity and the risk together

    The current price, targets, and stop loss define the setup before you decide whether it fits your own plan.

  • 3

    Understand what created the signal

    The strategy label identifies which MarketSloth logic detected the opportunity.

  • 4

    Get supporting context and timing

    Volume, MACD, RSI, ATR, and the timestamp provide additional context around the market conditions when the signal appeared.

Market Monitoring

The alert is just the start.

MarketSloth keeps tracking the setup after it fires — until the outcome is recorded.

  1. Developing

    A setup is forming. No alert yet.

  2. Confirmed

    The setup qualifies. Alert sent.

  3. Tracked live

    Targets and stop loss are monitored.

  4. Resolved

    Target hit or loss recorded.

  5. Recorded

    The outcome is added to the signal history.

Every signal is followed through to an outcome — whether you take the trade or not.

Trust

Every signal leaves a record.

MarketSloth tracks each signal through resolution — from alert to recorded outcome.

The goal is simple: measure signals the same way they were sent, not just the ones that worked. As our live history grows, those outcomes become the foundation for transparent performance reporting.

  • signals timestamped when generated
  • targets and stop losses tracked after the alert
  • wins and losses kept in the record
  • performance measured from the full signal history
